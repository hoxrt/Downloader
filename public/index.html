FAQ

Common Questions

Is VideoVault completely free to use?

Yes, VideoVault is 100% free. There are no hidden charges, premium tiers, or download limits. Just paste a URL and download.

Do I need to create an account?

No account, no registration, no email required. VideoVault works instantly — just visit the site and start downloading.

What video quality options are available?

Quality options depend on the source video. We show all available formats from the platform, ranging from SD (360p) up to 4K Ultra HD. For YouTube, you can often get 1080p or higher.

Can I download TikTok videos without a watermark?

VideoVault downloads the original source video directly via yt-dlp, which typically retrieves the watermark-free version where available through official TikTok servers.

How do I download only the audio as MP3?

After fetching your video, you'll see a "Download MP3" button under the "Audio Only" section. This extracts and converts the best-quality audio stream into an MP3 file.

Are private or age-restricted videos supported?

Private videos cannot be downloaded as they require account authentication. Age-restricted content is also not supported for the same reason. Only publicly accessible videos work.
